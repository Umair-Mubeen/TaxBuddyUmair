⚡ Quick Answer

Under Section 153, a withholding agent deducts tax when paying for goods (5%–5.5% filer), services (7% specified / 4% IT / 15% professionals / 14% other) or a contract (7.5%–8% filer). Non-filers generally pay double (Tenth Schedule). Select your transaction below.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is Section 153 withholding tax?
It is advance/withholding income tax that a "prescribed person" (withholding agent) deducts when paying for the sale of goods, rendering of services, or execution of a contract, and deposits it with FBR on the payee's behalf.

What is the withholding tax on services under 153(1)(b)?
Specified services (transport, courier, hotel, car rental, engineering and 21 more) are 7% for filers; IT & IT-enabled services 4%; independent professionals 15%; media advertising 1.5%; terminal/port operating 12%; and any other service 14%. Non-filers pay double.

How much tax is deducted on a contract?
Execution of a contract is 7.5% for a company, 8% for an individual/AOP, and 15% for a sportsperson (filer rates; non-filers double).

Is there a minimum amount before tax is deducted?
Yes — goods payments up to Rs 75,000 and services up to Rs 30,000 in a tax year (in aggregate) are not subject to deduction.

Is 153 tax final or adjustable?
For most taxpayers it is minimum tax; for companies on goods and listed-company contracts it can be adjustable. Check your specific status.
