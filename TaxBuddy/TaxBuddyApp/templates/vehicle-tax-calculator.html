⚡ Quick Answer

When you buy/register a new car in Pakistan, you pay advance income tax under Section 231B — from 0.5% to 12% of the vehicle's value for filers (by engine capacity), and 3× that for non-filers. Every year you also pay token tax under Section 234 (Rs 800 to Rs 10,000 for filers; double for non-filers). Transferring a car carries a separate fixed tax that drops 10% each year and becomes nil after 5 years.

Frequently Asked Questions

How much tax do I pay when buying a new car in Pakistan?
You pay advance income tax under Section 231B — 0.5% to 12% of the vehicle's value depending on engine capacity (a filer), or 3× that as a non-filer — collected at registration, plus provincial registration/number-plate fees.

Is the registration tax a percentage or a fixed amount?
For the current year it is a percentage of the vehicle's value across all engine-capacity bands (0.5%–12% for filers). Older fixed-rupee tables have been replaced.

How much higher is tax for a non-filer?
Registration and transfer tax are 3× the filer rate; the annual token tax is 2× the filer amount.

Do I pay transfer tax on an old car?
Transfer tax falls 10% for every year since first registration and becomes nil after 5 years. Cars up to 850cc are exempt from transfer tax.

Can I avoid the higher non-filer rate?
Yes — get on the Active Taxpayers List by filing your return before you register or transfer the vehicle. Use our ATL Check to confirm your filer status.
