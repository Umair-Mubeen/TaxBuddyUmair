PTA Mobile Tax Calculator — Pakistan 2026-27

📅 FBR Tax Year 2026-27 ✅ Official FBR Valuation 🛂 Passport vs CNIC
Quick answer: PTA mobile tax in Pakistan (2026-27) depends on your phone's C&F value in US dollars, and is lower if you register on your passport within 60 days of arrival (income tax exempt) than on your CNIC. For example, an iPhone 13 costs about Rs 22,000 on passport and Rs 23,000 on CNIC, while an iPhone 15 Pro Max runs about Rs 61,000 on passport and Rs 73,000 on CNIC. The tax is set by FBR (not PTA) and combines regulatory duty, sales tax, mobile handset levy and — on CNIC — income tax under Section 148.

❓ Frequently asked questions

Is PTA tax cheaper on passport or CNIC?
Registering on your passport within 60 days of arriving in Pakistan is cheaper because the income tax under Section 148 is waived. Registering on CNIC adds that income tax on top — from Rs 70 on cheap phones to Rs 11,500 on phones above $500.
Where do the phone values come from?
The USD values in the model list are FBR's own assessed customs (C&F) values from Valuation Ruling No. 2040/2026 issued by the Directorate General of Customs Valuation. These apply to used/older models; the newest flagships are valued separately, so enter their value manually.
Why do different websites show different PTA tax?
Because the final tax depends on the FBR customs value of your exact model and the USD exchange rate on the day you pay. This calculator uses the official FBR valuation and tax formula, but the only exact figure is the one generated by FBR DIRBS when you register.
How many phones can I register tax-free on passport?
You can register one mobile device tax-free per year under the passenger baggage allowance if it accompanies you; any additional device is taxed. Passport registration must be done within 60 days of arrival to keep the income-tax exemption.
What happens if I don't register my phone?
A non-PTA-compliant phone gets blocked from Pakistani mobile networks after a grace period (around 60 days), so it will only work on Wi-Fi. Paying the PTA tax and registering unblocks it permanently.
