How to Check ATL Status

1
Visit FBR IRIS Portal
Go to iris.fbr.gov.pk — FBR's official IRIS portal
2
Click "Online Verifications"
On the IRIS homepage scroll to Online Verifications — no login required
3
Select "Active Taxpayer List (Income Tax)"
From the left panel pick Active Taxpayer List (Income Tax) — the form opens on the right
4
Select Parameter Type
Choose NTN, CNIC, Passport No. or Reg/Inc. No., then enter your number
5
Enter Captcha & Verify
Enter the captcha and click Verify — result shows instantly: Active Filer ✅ or Non-Filer ❌

📝 How to Become a Filer

  1. Register on IRIS — iris.fbr.gov.pk
  2. Obtain your NTN (National Tax Number)
  3. File your income tax return for the latest tax year
  4. Your name appears on the ATL within 2–3 days of filing
Return filing deadline (Tax Year 2025): 31 October 2026
Statutory due date 30 September (Section 118, ITO 2001) — extended by FBR notification.

⚠️ Late Filer ATL Surcharge (Section 182A)

File after the due date (31 October 2026 for Tax Year 2025, as extended by FBR) and you can still join the ATL as a Late Filer by paying an ATL surcharge under Section 182A. Late filers appear on the list but pay intermediate WHT rates — higher than active filers, lower than non-filers.
